[01] Tectonic Manifesto & Materiality

A coherent tectonic whole is born from purpose, light, and materiality.

Under the leadership of Gustavo Massimino, Mass Arquitetura operates at the intersection of theoretical rigor and construction precision. Every volume, structural joint, and window aperture responds to site-specific environmental geometry.

We reject transient stylistic trends in favor of timeless materials: fair-faced concrete, raw timber, structural steel, and glass planes that dissolve spatial boundaries.

MAT.01

Fair-Faced Concrete

Monolithic structural mass with high thermal inertia and exposed formwork texture.

MAT.02

Glulam Timber

Engineered glue-laminated timber beams offering sustainable spans and tactile warmth.

MAT.03

Structural Glazing

Low-E floor-to-ceiling glass pockets that disappear into masonry walls.

MAT.04

Anodized Steel

Modular structural framing systems engineered for construction speed and spatial flexibility.

[03] Leadership & Academic Rigor

Gustavo Massimino

Founding Principal & Master of Architecture (M.Arch)

With a Master’s Degree in Architectural Design from the prestigious Faculty of Architecture and Urbanism at the University of São Paulo (FAU-USP) and post-graduate studies in Project Management at Business School São Paulo (BSP), Gustavo Massimino combines theoretical depth with rigorous execution.

Having collaborated with leading Brazilian design studios including Roberto Migotto, Studio Galvez De Vitto, Piratininga Arquitetos Associados, and Miguel Góes, Gustavo founded Mass Arquitetura in 2009. He served as Regional Director for CAU/SP and has taught architectural design at university level.
